About

Creative by instinct.
Technical by choice.

I'm Macdonald Opara — a brand designer, visual creative, and developer based in Lagos. For over a decade I've been shaping brands and crafting visuals that make businesses impossible to ignore.

Along the way, I learned to code — and now I build the things I design. That's a rare combination. Most designers hand off to developers. Most developers don't care about brand. I sit in the middle, and that's where the best work happens.

From Shopify stores to SaaS products, I close the gap between how a brand looks and how it performs. Whether it's an identity system, a motion design package, or a full web build — I bring the same obsessive attention to craft to every project.

I work with AI as a core part of my production pipeline — not as a shortcut, but as a force multiplier that lets me deliver premium results, faster. From AI-enhanced product photography to intelligent design workflows, I use the latest tools to give clients output that used to cost 10— more.

Beyond screens, I design corporate presentations, pitch decks, and brand reports — in PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides, and Canva. I also build Canva template systems and brand kits that keep teams on-brand without needing a designer every time. Visual communication, whether it lives in a browser or a boardroom, is what I do.

How I Work
