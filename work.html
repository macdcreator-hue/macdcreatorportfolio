E-commerce Shopify AI Creative Fashion

St.Cassie

Fashion & Retail — Lagos, Nigeria

The Problem

A premium brand with no digital presence — and only phone photos.

St.Cassie had no e-commerce presence. Their only product images were raw phone photos — making a premium fashion label look unprofessional and costing them online sales entirely. The brand equity was real. The digital execution wasn't there.

The Solution

A Shopify store built from scratch, with AI-transformed product visuals.

I built their Shopify store from the ground up and used AI to transform raw phone photos into polished, professional product visuals. Launched with fully optimised product listings ready to convert from day one.

Deliverables

  • Full Shopify store build
  • AI-enhanced product photography (from phone shots)
  • Optimised product listings and copywriting
  • Complete e-commerce launch and handover

Result

"A premium fashion store that looked like it cost 10— more — live and ready to sell."

Web Design React Healthcare

Negoncare

Healthcare — ENT Clinic — Lagos, Nigeria

The Problem

A specialist clinic with no strong digital presence — missing patient trust online.

Negoncare, a specialist ENT clinic in Lagos, had no strong digital presence. They were missing the opportunity to build trust with patients online and convert website visitors into booked appointments. In healthcare, trust is the product — and they had no platform to establish it.

The Solution

Premium web presence with trust-first design and clear patient journeys.

I designed and built a premium web presence combining trust-building design language with clear patient journeys — making it easy for visitors to understand the clinic's services and take the next step. Built with React and Tailwind for performance and maintainability.

Deliverables

  • Full website design (Figma)
  • React + Tailwind CSS implementation
  • Patient journey UX architecture
  • Brand-aligned visual design system

Status

Live — rebuild currently in progress with an improved stack and enhanced patient experience.
